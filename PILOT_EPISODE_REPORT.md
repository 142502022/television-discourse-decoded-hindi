## Limitations

- Diarization labels need not map to people; no speaker exclusions were applied.
- Overlap candidates are detector-reconciled, not human-verified; `case=AGREE` is detector corroboration, never semantic agreement.
- Interruptions are OSD-derived operational attempts; topic labels and changes are model outputs.
- Topic windows are coarse; interruption associations are candidate, not causal.

Artifact-specific limitations:
- English model baseline; non-English artifacts are filtered by default.
- ASR/diarization errors propagate into topic windows.
- Topic labels are model candidates and require human review.
- Topic boundaries and hierarchy are not validated against annotations.
- Topic windows are coarse (~tens of seconds); changes are discrete run boundaries (audit L5).
- Interruptions are the OSD-only list; reconciled overlap candidates are not included (audit L7).
- Nested and duplicate events are unioned before counting to avoid double counting (audit L6).
- Proximity is not causation; small event counts give unstable rates.
- Topic labels and timeline come from an English model and inherit ASR/diarization errors.
- Re-derivation shares the raw final.json schema and configured thresholds with the summary.
- Nested/duplicate overlap candidates collapse in the union but persist in source counts (audit L6).
- Agreement/disagreement remain unavailable without annotations or a validated classifier.
- A matched metric confirms arithmetic agreement, not semantic correctness of the underlying detector.
